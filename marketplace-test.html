🎮 How to Access Marketplace:

  1. Open the main game at http://localhost:3001
  2. Look for the "🏪 Market" button in the action buttons section
  3. Click the button to open the marketplace modal
  4. Sell your harvestable crops, ore, and wood items!

💰 Selling Items:

  • Crops: Harvestable crops from your farm plots
  • Ore: Mined copper, silver, gold, and diamonds
  • Wood: Harvested oak and pine wood
  • Quality Bonus: Items with 80%+ quality get 20% price bonus
